The name

Not just an apartment.
A piece of Sardinia
to live in.

The pintadera is the ancient Nuragic seal: a hand-carved terracotta stamp used to press a mark that could be recognised among a thousand. Identity, belonging, authenticity.

We named this holiday home in Uta after it because every room bears a mark you won't find anywhere else. We didn't just furnish it: we brought it back to life.

Look up

Painted ceilings
no one makes anymore

Floral in the kitchen, geometric and gilded in the bedroom. Seventy years ago someone painted them by hand, one by one, in this Campidano country house. We restored them with care and left them exactly where they have always been. Sleep, have breakfast, look up: it's a detail you won't forget.

The story

It was the home of
Antonio and Maddalena

This is where Antonio Lecca and Maddalena Carboni raised their children and watched their grandchildren grow up. A house built in ladiri — the sun-dried mud bricks of the Campidano — with the care for detail that old houses used to have.

Today it belongs to Alessandro and Roberta, who restored it room by room without betraying its soul. This isn't an anonymous rental: it's a place with memories, where you can still breathe authentic Sardinia.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know first

Do I need a car?

Within 500 metres you'll find everything you need on foot: supermarket, pharmacy, tobacconist, café, bus stop, schools, town hall, park, bakery and greengrocer. You'll want a car to go beyond Uta: Cagliari airport and port, the city centre, cinemas, the sea and the mountains are 15-20 minutes away, with plenty more to discover on day trips. All in all it's a convenient, strategic base for exploring the whole south of Sardinia: 15-20 minutes from everything, but away from the crowds and at more reasonable prices.

Is check-in self-service?

It's flexible: whenever possible Alessandro or Roberta will welcome you in person, otherwise we arrange a self check-in. Message us on WhatsApp to agree on your arrival time.

Are pets allowed?

We don't accept pets at the moment.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am. Different times can be arranged depending on availability.

What is the cancellation policy?

Flexible cancellation, with the exact conditions shown in the rate you choose on Booking or Airbnb.

How many guests can the apartment host?

It's designed for two guests, with a double bed, equipped kitchen and a 40 m² private veranda.

Is there a minimum stay?

Yes, the minimum stay is 2 nights.

Can I stay for work or for longer periods?

Yes. For stays longer than a week we offer custom rates on request: message us on WhatsApp.

Is there a washing machine and dryer?

Yes, the apartment has a washing machine and a dryer for guests to use.

Where can I park?

Parking is free and easy to find along the street.

Is there air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes: there are two Wi-Fi-controlled air conditioners and fibre internet.

Is there a tourist tax?

No, Uta does not charge a tourist tax.

Can I book directly, without Booking or Airbnb?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to book directly with us, with no platform fees, and we'll reply within an hour.
